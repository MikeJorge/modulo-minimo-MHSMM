What the figure establishes

  • mm-a1 is the sole mutable non-admin execution identity; mm-review remains independent read-only.

  • The primary Gate workspace is bounded within scratch/; global scratch is not generally writable.
  • Identity bindings are non-directional; read/write/evidence accesses are typed directed flows.
  • Toolchain, credentials/configuration and child network capability remain separated.
